## 1. Reddit — r/codex（首选，最精准）

**Title**
`I tracked every Codex global reset for a month — the "announced vs actually landed" gap is the whole problem`

**Body**
```
Every global Codex reset starts with a post, and then there's a gap — sometimes minutes, sometimes hours — before the allowance actually refreshes. Most trackers just record "a reset was announced", which is how people end up refreshing /usage at 2am for nothing.

So I built one that keeps three states apart: announced, pending, verified. Only verified counts toward the reset count, and the history page shows what was claimed vs what landed.

What's in it:
- last verified global reset + 24h / 48h probability (computed from the actual interval history, not vibes)
- 28-day day-by-day plan (which days were resets, which were only releases)
- monthly archives, plus the 5-hour window vs weekly cap distinction explained
- no login, no account access — it only reads public announcements and the record

It's a solo project, Chinese-first with English under /en/. Feedback on what's missing is welcome — especially if you know of a reset I missed in the record.

https://codex-reset.opentunnel.asia/
```

> 注意：r/codex 版规通常允许工具分享，但发之前扫一眼置顶规则；不要在标题里写 "free tool"，容易被判 self-promo。
